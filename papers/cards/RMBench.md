# RMBench: Memory-Dependent Robotic Manipulation Benchmark with Insights into Policy Design

## Navigation
- Paper note: [RMBench: Memory-Dependent Robotic Manipulation Benchmark with Insights into Policy Design note](../notes/rmbench-note.md)

## Core Insight

- RMBench 包含 9 个双臂操作任务，涵盖不同级别的任务记忆复杂性，能够对机器人操作中的记忆保持和利用进行大规模和可控的研究
- 提出Task Memory Complexity衡量任务对记忆的需求
- 提出MEM-0，一种长短期记忆解决方案
